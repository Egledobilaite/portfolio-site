# About Me

For eight years I worked as a sustainability consultant. I helped businesses understand and meet their legal sustainability requirements, so they could stay compliant without getting lost in the details. I also helped companies build a real culture of sustainability, one where people care about it and act on it, not just a policy on paper. A big part of my work was teaching: I ran training for teams and explained complex topics in simple, practical terms.

## A new direction

I recently decided to try a new career path. I'm now learning to build software by directing AI agents. I plan what needs to be built, break it into clear steps, guide the agents through the work, and check that the result does what it should.

I am interested in creating my own brand of sustainability oriented products that are healthy for people and planet.

## What I bring

- **Turning complex rules into clear actions.** Years of working with regulations taught me to take something complicated and make it workable.
- **Helping people change how they work.** Building sustainability culture meant getting people on board with new habits, which matters for any new tool or process.
- **Clear communication.** Teaching teams taught me to explain things plainly and to listen to what people actually need.

This site is where I share what I'm building as I learn.
